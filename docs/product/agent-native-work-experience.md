# Agent-native Work experience

Sofie is the primary Digital Worker and coordinator. Agents are persistent specialists with their own identity, purpose, capabilities and limits. Work is durable execution; Result is its retained outcome; Proof explains the evidence. MyFactory produces software candidates, Relay governs communication/capabilities, and Environment Fabric determines where admitted Work executes. These identities and responsibilities are distinct.

## Conversation

A conversation displays Work associated by retained server context, with current status, Result, expandable Proof and the canonical publication decision. Owner choice → review → confirm still binds one exact candidate. Continuing a conversation does not reuse an earlier Work's authority. A conversational fork does not copy a Work association. Older Work remains available through bounded history pages.

## Agent home

Open Agents to see purpose, associated Work and Results, responsibilities, recent tasks and conversations. Model settings and instructions are under Advanced; capability access stays explicit. Configuration status (active/paused/etc.) is not presence. Operational status comes from retained Work; a failed refresh never claims Idle. The newest 10 Works and 20 conversations/tasks/responsibilities are shown, with existing history/search available for older records.

Duplicate configuration creates a fresh identity with instructions and preferences. Capabilities require review on the copy. Credentials, Memory, approvals and active Work do not transfer.

## Responsibilities and collaboration

Routines represent ongoing responsibilities; schedules determine their triggers. Existing execution_routines and reminder admission remain canonical. At this checkpoint background execution is disabled and is shown as Waiting, not Monitoring. General laptop-independent operation is not qualified.

Groups will represent explicit collaboration around an objective; Relay remains the messaging plane. No Group inherits its members' combined authority. Persistent Group coordination and real multi-agent handoff are still implementation/qualification work, not shipped claims.
