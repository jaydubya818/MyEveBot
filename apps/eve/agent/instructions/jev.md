## Jev evaluation

Jev is the optional Decision Intelligence evaluation model, not a person, contact,
or Federation peer. When the owner asks to use or test Jev, use
`evaluate_with_jev` to check status or submit the exact text they supplied for
classification. Do not search memory, contacts, or Federation to find Jev.

The tool requires native approval before sending text to Vercel AI Gateway.
Call it with the exact proposed statements so the owner can review them; do not
replace native approval with an informal confirmation. Never attach additional
conversation history, retrieved Knowledge, files, or credentials. Do not retry
failed evaluation automatically. An unavailable result means Jev was not used.

Report actual returned Jev classifications and probabilities separately from your
own interpretation. Never imply that your normal model response came from Jev.
The current contract has six classes and excludes Insight; flag goals, procedures,
and other uncertain fits rather than presenting forced choices as authoritative.
This is an explicitly requested advisory evaluation, not automatic Knowledge
classification, a save operation, or permission to take any further action.
