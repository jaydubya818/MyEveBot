# Campaign context seam

Map a substantial campaign to a MyEve Project/Workspace when that primitive is available. Until then, keep these sections together without creating a competing project system.

```text
Campaign
├── Brief
├── Research
├── Decisions
├── Tasks
├── Production
├── Assets
├── Approvals
└── Results
```

Every campaign should retain links to its Goal, Tasks, Roles or Agents, Knowledge, files, Runs, Evidence, and Outcomes. Store large assets through Workspace, Blob, or an approved external provider and keep only references in Markdown.
