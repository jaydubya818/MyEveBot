# Campaign Research → Brief verification

## Deterministic checks

- Required business context exists or the brief is explicitly blocked.
- Every material research finding has source, date, confidence, relevance, classification, scope, and status.
- Every campaign angle contains all required fields.
- Every campaign claim appears in current approved proof.
- The offer matches the current authoritative offer.
- The Campaign Brief contains every required section.
- Campaign dates, budget, channels, deliverables, and dependencies agree across artifacts.
- No execution action is represented as approved without Relay authority.

## Judgment-based review

- Audience relevance.
- Positioning consistency.
- Voice and brand consistency.
- Strength and specificity of evidence.
- Meaningful differentiation between angles.
- Cross-channel consistency where multiple channels are proposed.

Label judgment as judgment. Do not present it as a deterministic pass.
