# Positive Work release obligation

This release authorizes chat only. Its canonical model, executable-tool,
feature, instruction and accounting controls deliberately deny paid Work and
Factory execution. Configuration or client text cannot enable them.

The required Golden, E3 and independent jobs retain their positive Work
obligations. Their new source-bound precondition exits nonzero with
`BLOCKED_NOT_QUALIFIED` before dependency installation, application database
creation, builds or provider setup. The CI PostgreSQL service can already exist;
the precondition never connects to it. The jobs remain failed, not skipped or
allowed to fail. Original downstream Work assertions stay in place and are
not qualified when failure prevents those steps from running.

`positive-work-v1-archive.json` pins the ten original campaign bodies to
95cbd216d09724b1a0e346e7076c92aae99cb09b and its tree. Those files remain
byte-identical at their original paths; Git preserves that historical version.
The manifest grants no authority and does not convert historical evidence into
current qualification. Source/profile/archive/guard drift remains blocked and
requires separate review. There is no successful or synthetic Work mode in this
precondition. Unknown observations also exit nonzero using fixed public codes.

This is a setup-diagnosis correction, not a chat behavior pass, proof of an
earlier runtime failure's cause, or full beta qualification. A separately
authorized and qualified Work release is required to satisfy positive Work.
No required check is removed, renamed or replaced with a chat-only result.

Full chat-runner adoption remains deferred: CI operator/source/cluster binding,
physical Eve CLI and browser dependencies, bounded PID settlement and overall
deadlines, artifact allowlisting and multiple-child diagnostic ownership need
review. No adapter, runner change, fixture bypass, trigger suppression,
production activation, spending-limit change or new execution service is added.
